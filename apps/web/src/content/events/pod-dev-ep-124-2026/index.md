## en

It's always rewarding to share experiences that started as an experiment and grew into something much bigger.

I had the opportunity to talk about how our team prototyped a groundbreaking AI solution at a hackathon, Bifrost, which eventually evolved into a heavily used tool at Hotmart, with more than 600 PRs merged and migrations happening company-wide.

Guilherme Dantas and I joined forces with Jéssica Betônico and AWS's Breno Gibson to tell this story in this incredible Pod&Dev episode (in PT-BR).

I hope you learn something from our experience, and I hope to come back soon to share more of what we're building.

Exciting things to come!

## pt

É sempre gratificante compartilhar experiências que começaram como um experimento e viraram algo muito maior.

Eu e o Guilherme Dantas nos juntamos à Jéssica Betônico e ao Breno Gibson, da AWS, para contar essa história neste episódio incrível do Pod&Dev.
