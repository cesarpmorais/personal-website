## en

An unforgettable week at Hotmart Fire, where we had the opportunity to announce Launchpad, our new product that aims to revolutionize the Creator Economy!

For me, this was especially meaningful because I've been working on Launchpad over the past few months, alongside an incredible team that has been teaching me something new every day. But even more special was getting the chance to demonstrate what we've built to our customers and have that real, human connection with the people who are using their work to impact thousands of lives every day.

And then comes the excitement for what lies ahead: not only for the Creator Economy, but for technology as a whole. Seeing brilliant use cases for AI like this one, and how they can empower anyone to create and make a real impact, makes me incredibly excited about the future.
