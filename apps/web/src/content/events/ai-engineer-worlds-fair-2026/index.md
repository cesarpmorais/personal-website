## en

If technology is a universe, AI is a black hole. The more you learn, the more you realize how much there is still to explore: harnesses, evals, context engineering... one topic naturally leads to another. Keeping up isn't easy. The paradigm shifts in a matter of hours, startups emerge to solve problems that didn't exist last week, and getting lost is simply part of the process.

That's why attending the AI Engineer World's Fair was such a remarkable experience. The event brings together some of the world's leading AI companies, researchers, and engineers, creating a unique space to exchange ideas, discuss the latest challenges, and showcase what's being built at the frontier of AI.

In an ecosystem that changes every day, it gave me the rarest thing of all: context. It felt like an Alexandria Library for the AI era, updated in real time, with the people building these ideas sharing their experiences and perspectives.

In the end, it was an opportunity to witness a computing revolution from the inside while it's still being built.

A huge thank you to Hotmart for the trust and for giving me the opportunity to represent the company at such a special event — and to my fellow mentors who helped me throughout the journey: Guilherme Dantas, Jônatas Renan Camilo Alves, Matt Montenegro and Tiago Cunha Fernandes.
